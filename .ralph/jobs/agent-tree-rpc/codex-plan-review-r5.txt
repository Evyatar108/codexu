### Completeness

[Medium] The dedup/metadata-merge rule can leave live clients with stale optional metadata. If v2 `node-added` emits first with `nickname`/`role` null, then legacy spawn-end later enriches the reducer snapshot without an emitted delta, already-mounted clients will not learn the richer metadata until they re-fetch. Either add a lightweight metadata/status delta for this merge or explicitly accept that live metadata may lag until snapshot refresh.
