### Feasibility

[Medium] `connection.connectionType === 'session-scoped'` in [sessionUpdateHandler.ts](/D:/harness-efforts/codexu/packages/happy-server/sources/app/api/socket/sessionUpdateHandler.ts) is weaker than the plan’s “spoofing impossible” claim.
