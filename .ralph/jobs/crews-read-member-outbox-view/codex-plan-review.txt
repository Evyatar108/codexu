**Feasibility**
- [High] D-002 is feasible, but the delegation plan is underspecified in a way that can break `read-member-reports --help` and error text. `hooks/commands/runtime.js` uses `surface.usage || command.usage` for CLI help, so a module that “re-exports read-member’s surfaces” from `hooks/commands/read-member.js` will keep the old `node read-member.js ...` / `/crews:read-member` usage unless it clones and overrides `surfaces.cli.usage`. The reused parser/formatter also hard-codes `/read-member` in usage/errors. The D-002 amendment is directionally adequate, but it needs explicit wrapper/clone guidance for command-specific usage while sharing the handler/filter logic.

Changing `read-member` usage/description and adding `read-member-reports` will require updating that golden; otherwise the full `tests/run.js` suite will fail.
