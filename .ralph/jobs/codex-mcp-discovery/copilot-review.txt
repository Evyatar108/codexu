### Non-blocking

1. **Unknown-field passthrough collides with codex's `deny_unknown_fields`** — `packages/happy-cli/src/codex/projectMcpConfig.ts:11,16,29`. Both `StdioEntry`, `HttpEntry`, and the root use `.passthrough()`, but `RawMcpServerConfig` in `codex/external/repos/codex-patched/codex-rs/config/src/mcp_types.rs` is `#[schemars(deny_unknown_fields)]`. A user typo like `commnd` or a Claude-Code-only key (e.g. a future field) silently leaves our loader and then fails `thread/start` server-side with an opaque deserialize error rather than the structured per-entry skip + `logger.warn` the plan promises. Recommend tightening the schemas to the exact codex `RawMcpServerConfig` field set (or at minimum the documented stdio/http subsets) and treating unknowns as a per-entry validation failure routed through the existing `logger.warn` path.

2. **HTTP `url` is `z.string()` rather than `z.string().url()`** — `projectMcpConfig.ts:14`. Empty string or `"not a url"` passes the loader and is sent to codex, which then either rejects the thread or attempts a malformed connection. Cheap to fix and matches the "skip malformed entries" intent.

3. **Reserved-name check is case-sensitive on the literal `'happy'`** — `projectMcpConfig.ts:56`. `Happy`, `HAPPY`, or `" happy"` would slip past the guard, land in `projectMcpServers`, and then be overwritten only because the spread order favors the bridge. The bridge survives, but the project's similarly-named entry is silently dropped without a warn explaining why. Either lowercase-compare or also warn for confusable variants.
