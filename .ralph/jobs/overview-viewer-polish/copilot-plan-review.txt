The plan's acceptance criterion "file:// build opens cleanly" is correct but the autonomous executor needs an explicit "open `plans/overview.html` via file://, open the popover, hit Escape, assert no console errors" step.

`useHashNav` currently calls `expandedControls.setTaskExpanded`.
