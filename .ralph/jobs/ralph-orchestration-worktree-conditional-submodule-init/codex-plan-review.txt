[High] The env override undermines the “impl-phase safety net stays in place” claim. `convert-to-ralph-prd` calls `worktree-create.mjs` for impl worktrees at [SKILL.md](/mnt/d/harness-efforts/codexu/ai-developer-toolkit/plugins/ralph/skills/convert-to-ralph-prd/SKILL.md:357), and `runSubmoduleInit` is shared at [worktree-create.mjs](/mnt/d/harness-efforts/codexu/ai-developer-toolkit/plugins/ralph/src/worktree-create.mjs:259).
