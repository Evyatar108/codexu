### Technical Constraints

sccache works as `RUSTC_WRAPPER` and supports Rust plus clang/MSVC-style C/C++ compilation. Its Rust docs matter: Cargo-style rustc invocations are cacheable, but crates invoking the system linker (`bin`, `dylib`, `cdylib`, `proc-macro`) are not cached; linker steps and the final `codex-core` link remain expensive. Rust incremental must be disabled, so set `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0`.

Do not oversell “env-var thrash” resilience. Changing `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS` should not affect rustc cache keys. Changing `LTO`, `codegen-units`, or `RUSTFLAGS=-C debuginfo=0` changes compiler arguments and should be expected to miss or require a versioned cache/profile. The real fix is sccache plus a frozen profile, not sccache alone.

`RUSTY_V8_ARCHIVE` points at a prebuilt `.lib`; sccache will not cache the archive itself, but it should coexist with it because Cargo/rustc consume it during build/link. Verification should prove no wrapper/linker/path issue with `clang-cl`, `lld-link`, xwin `LIB`/`INCLUDE`, and the archive.
