[High] `scoreRecommendations(byTaskId, overviewData, config?)` cannot implement the planned dependency-state score as a pure function. The score needs PRD `userStories[].dependencies[]` and `passes`, but `OverviewRalphState.byTaskId` only has aggregate `storyCompletion`. Add `prdsByTaskId` to the function signature or drop that scoring dimension. Relevant files: `scripts/lib/sync-core.mjs`, `.ralph/jobs/*/prd.json`.

[High] The `writeSidecar` hook is not sufficient as described. Current `writeSidecar({ repoRoot, config, state })` has no access to PRDs, bundle `createdAt`/`completedAt`, or `overviewData`; those are discarded by `assembleStateFromBundles`.
