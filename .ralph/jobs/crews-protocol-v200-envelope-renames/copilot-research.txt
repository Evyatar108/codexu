### Technical Constraints

The schema is intentionally strict in tests but warn-only in production. Existing `.crews` JSONL history may contain old-shape rows, so a hard read cutoff would make old inbox history less readable even if new writes are correct. There is no TypeScript type layer; schema enforcement is centralized in `hooks\protocol\envelope.js`.
