### Feasibility

[Critical] **L3b’s fail-open write budget is too broad for protocol-critical state.** The plan explicitly threads hook-context single-attempt/fail-open behavior into `consumeMailbox` manifest writes, but `hooks/mailbox.js::consumeMailbox` appends inbox history, empties `mailbox.json`, then updates `lastInboxSeq` / `lastReviewRequiredSeq`. If that manifest update fails open, mail can be durably consumed while the review cursor/gate is stale or wrong. Limit fail-open to observability/advisory writes; protocol-state writes must fail loud or remain transactional.

[High] **Heartbeat stamps need an ownership check, not just freshness.** A lock-free `<actorDir>/heartbeat` stamp with `{ ts, pid, epoch }` must be ignored unless its `pid` and `epoch` match the manifest’s current `lastListenerPid` / `lastListenerEpoch`. Otherwise an orphan/superseded listener can keep writing a fresh stamp and mask a dead current listener, weakening the v3.6.2 epoch ownership invariant.

[Medium] **`deriveListenerState(manifest)` cannot read a per-actor stamp as planned.** The current pure helper only receives a manifest and is directly tested/called that way; path-aware code lives in `getListenerState(name, crew, stateCwd, opts)`. The plan should route stamp reads through the path-aware helper or explicitly change the helper signature and all direct tests/callers.
